POLICY DOCUMENT: Auto and Home Insurance Services
Document ID: POL-INS-001
Last Updated: 2025-01-01

--- CLAIMS FILING AND TIMELINE ---
Customers should file a claim as soon as possible after an incident. For auto insurance, an adjuster must make first contact within 3 business days of the claim being filed. For home insurance, an adjuster must be dispatched within 5 business days. A final claim decision must be made within 30 days of receiving all required documentation. If additional information is needed, the customer must be notified within 10 days of the claim being filed. If a claim decision has not been issued within 45 days without a documented reason, the customer has the right to escalate to the state insurance commissioner.

--- REPAIR ESTIMATES AND OEM PARTS ---
For auto claims, customers have the right to choose their own repair shop. The bank's insurer will provide an independent estimate; if the customer's chosen shop quotes a higher price, a supplemental review will be initiated. Customers may request Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) parts in lieu of aftermarket parts; this may result in an additional cost to the customer depending on the policy terms. The insurer must document in writing any reason for denying an OEM parts request.

--- RENTAL CAR COVERAGE ---
Rental car reimbursement coverage must be explicitly listed on the policy declarations page to be active. Agents are required to present rental coverage as an optional add-on at enrollment. If a customer was not offered rental coverage at enrollment, they may file a complaint with the customer relations department. Approved rental reimbursement begins the day after a covered vehicle is disabled and ends when repairs are complete or the total loss settlement is paid.

--- PREMIUM INCREASES AT RENEWAL ---
Premium increases at renewal must be accompanied by a written explanation of the factors contributing to the change. Acceptable factors include changes in the customer's claims history, changes in the vehicle's value, changes in the customer's geographic risk profile, and industry-wide rate adjustments. Rate increases of more than 25% require a 45-day advance notice. Customers who have filed no claims in the prior policy period and receive an increase of more than 15% may request a formal rate review.

--- CLAIM DENIALS AND APPEALS ---
If a claim is denied, the customer must receive a written denial letter within 5 business days of the decision. The letter must cite the specific policy provision that supports the denial. Customers have 60 days from the denial letter to file a formal appeal. The appeal must be reviewed by a supervisor not involved in the original decision. A response to the appeal must be provided within 30 days.

--- HOME INSURANCE SPECIFIC ---
Home insurance policies cover the structure of the home, attached structures, and personal property up to the limits defined in the policy. Water damage from sudden and accidental events (such as a burst pipe) is typically covered. Damage from gradual leaks or lack of maintenance is typically excluded. Mold remediation is covered only when it results directly from a covered water damage event. Customers who are renovating or adding to their home should notify their agent to ensure adequate coverage during construction.

--- POLICY MISREPRESENTATION ---
If a customer believes that the coverage they purchased does not match what was described by the agent at enrollment, they may file a misrepresentation complaint within 90 days of discovering the discrepancy. The bank must investigate and respond within 20 business days. If misrepresentation is confirmed, the customer is entitled to a retroactive policy correction and a refund of any premium overpayment.
